## Task 1 — Different Types of Cloud-Based Services (25 minutes)

Watch the following video on cloud hosting types:

- 📹 [Different Types of Cloud-Based Services — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PpX7TuR6DCY]

While watching, refer to **lesson.md Part 1** and pay attention to:
- The difference between PaaS, IaaS, and container hosting
- What each type of hosting abstracts away from the developer
- Why different teams choose different types of hosting
- Where databases fit into each hosting model

**Guiding Questions:**
1. In your own words, what is the main difference between PaaS and IaaS?
2. If a company wants full control over their server configuration, which type of hosting should they choose?
3. Why is container hosting particularly well-suited for modern DevOps workflows?

## Task 2 — Railway, Render, and Fly.io Comparison (25 minutes)

Watch the following video comparing popular hosting platforms:

- 📹 [Railway, Render, and Fly.io Comparison — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4T1rUkCfBFc]

While watching, refer to **lesson.md Part 2** and pay attention to:
- How each platform handles deployment
- The free tier limitations of each platform
- Which platform is easiest to get started with
- How cold starts affect user experience on free tiers

**Guiding Questions:**
1. What is a "cold start" and why does it matter for free tier applications?
2. Which platform would you choose for a learning project and why?
3. What is the key trade-off between Railway and Render on the free tier?

## Task 3 — Platform Selection Practice (20 minutes)

No video for this task. Refer to **lesson.md Part 5** and read through the four hosting scenario cards before class. For each scenario write down:

1. Which hosting platform you would recommend
2. Two reasons that justify your choice
3. One potential downside of your chosen platform for that scenario

Come to class with your answers ready — the activity will be done in groups, so having your own thoughts prepared first will help the discussion.

**Guiding Questions:**
1. Is budget always the deciding factor when choosing a hosting platform? What else matters?
2. What questions would you ask a client before recommending a hosting platform?
3. Why is it important to understand platform limitations (cold starts, credit limits, free tier expiry) before committing to one?
